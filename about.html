The artist

4dtruth is the studio of Seun Okunola, a multidisciplinary artist, colourist and colour therapist based in Lagos, Nigeria. Working in thick palette-knife oil, Seun builds crowds, markets, cityscapes and portraits out of colour and texture, where every figure is one stroke in a bigger story.

Practice

The work moves between scale and intimacy. Festival scenes like the Ojude series and the open markets of Lagos are made of thousands of single strokes, each one a person, an umbrella, a moment.

The portraits slow down: a head wrap, a silhouette against warm light, a study in red. Across all of it runs one idea: individual stories come together into a powerful collective rhythm.
