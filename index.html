About

PainterColouristColour therapistStrategistActivist

4dtruth is the studio of Seun Okunola, a multidisciplinary artist and colourist in Lagos, Nigeria, building crowds, markets and portraits out of colour and texture.

Thick, palette-knife oil on canvas. Festival scenes from the Ojude series, the open markets of Lagos, and quieter studies of light, identity and the beauty of togetherness.
