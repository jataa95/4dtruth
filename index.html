About

4dtruth is the studio of Seun Okunola, a multidisciplinary artist, colourist and colour therapist based in Lagos, Nigeria.

Working in thick, palette-knife oil on canvas, Seun builds crowds, markets, cityscapes and portraits out of colour and texture. Each figure is one stroke in a bigger story. The work moves between festival scenes like the Ojude series, the open markets of Lagos, and quieter studies of light and identity.

Beyond the canvas, Seun works as a strategist and activist, using colour to explore unity, resilience and the beauty of shared experience.
